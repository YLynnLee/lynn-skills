# Scope

This repo is the set of skills I build, use and write about. It is curated.

## The bar

An idea or feedback issue stays open only if it clears both parts:

1. **Observed failure.** Something went wrong in a real session: the skill misfired, produced the wrong thing, or broke on a setup it claims to support.
2. **Fits the skill.** It isn't in `.out-of-scope/`, isn't a past rejection, and isn't a config option or a tweak to suit one person's workflow.

## By bucket

- **Promoted buckets** (`design/`, …) and `in-progress/`: judged against the bar.
- **`misc/`**: frozen and unmaintained. Issues are closed.
- **New skills**: ideas are welcome in Discussions; contributions are closed.

## Process

Unclear issues get one round of questions and the `needs-info` label, and close after 14 days with no reply. Rejected ideas close as "not planned", and recurring ones are recorded in `.out-of-scope/`:

- [single-agent-skills](./.out-of-scope/single-agent-skills.md)
