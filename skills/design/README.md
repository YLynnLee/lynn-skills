# Design

Design and front-end style work, and diagrams of how things work. Shipped in the `lynn-skills` plugin.

## User-invoked

None yet.

## Model-invoked

- **[reference-to-style](./reference-to-style/SKILL.md)**: Turn design references into a measured style, shown on a visual board and backed by a written guide, then apply it to your site when you ask. [Docs](../../docs/design/reference-to-style.md)
- **[flowmap](./flowmap/SKILL.md)**: Map how a codebase, system or process flows, with every box and arrow traced to its source, guesses drawn dashed, and paths that break a rule drawn red. [Docs](../../docs/design/flowmap.md)
